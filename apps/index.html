Installing Beta APKs on Android

Clean, direct installation without third-party app stores.

1

Download APK

Click the download button to save the official signed daily-v1.1-beta.apk file to your device.

2

Open Installer

Tap the download notification or locate the APK in your Android "Files" or "Downloads" directory.

3

Allow Source

If prompted by Android security, toggle "Allow from this source" for your browser or file manager.

4

Launch Daily

Tap "Install" and open Daily. Snap authentic moments with zero pressure and share with your inner circle.
