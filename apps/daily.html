Our Philosophy

NO FILTERS. NO FOLLOWER COUNTS. JUST REAL LIFE.

Most social apps demand curated perfection, endless public feeds, and vanity metrics. Daily is designed for authentic, in-the-moment sharing with the people who matter to you. Snap candid glimpses of your day, share them in a tactile card stack, and preserve your personal life chronicle.

Crafted Features

Engineered for Authenticity

Every feature is intentionally built around the experience of a disposable camera combined with modern cloud reliability.

Analog Shutter & Instant Queue

Shoot photos with vintage color grading, authentic mechanical shutter feedback, a 5-second undo safeguard, and non-blocking background uploads.

Physical Card Stack Feed

Browse your friends' daily exposures like a physical stack of photo prints. Swipe cards naturally across your screen and catch up before exposures expire in 24 hours.

Interactive Emoji Rain

React to friends' photos with vibrant emoji bursts that cascade down the screen in real-time, delivering playful and expressive feedback.

Monthly Calendar Vault

Your daily shots don't disappear into thin air. At midnight, every exposure you take safely archives into your private, searchable calendar vault.

Interactive 1:1 Homescreen Widget

Glance at your friends' latest moments directly from your home screen. Select from automated slideshows, pinned favorites, or friend takeover modes.

Fast QR Discovery & Story Cards

Add friends in seconds using the live QR scanner or import a screenshot directly from photos. Customize your personal QR poster with color themes and emojis.

Privacy & Complete Data Ownership

No algorithms, no third-party data tracking, and zero public follower feeds. Your memories live in an encrypted cloud vault. Export all your photos and data into a single ZIP archive, or permanently delete your account at any time with one tap.
