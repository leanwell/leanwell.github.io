Daily — Moments & Memories

Capture authentic moments on a retro camera roll shared with close friends.

Daily is an authentic daily photo-sharing app designed with the intentional feel of a retro disposable camera. Capture uncurated moments of your day, share them with close friends in a tactile card stack, and preserve your life chronicle in a private monthly calendar vault.

Optional, used exclusively for friend requests and daily capture reminders.

How to Install on Android

Installing signed APKs directly gives you immediate access to beta builds without app store delays.

1

Download APK

Click the download button to grab daily-v1.1-beta.apk (~7.3 MB) directly onto your phone.

2

Open the File

Tap the download completed notification in your notification tray or open your phone's "Downloads" folder.

3

Enable Permission

If prompted with "For security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings and toggle "Allow from this source".

4

Ready to Snap

Tap Install. Once completed, launch Daily, set your profile, and start taking authentic daily exposures!

Android Installation Note: "Allow from this source" is standard Android security for sideloaded applications. Daily's APK is officially signed and clean from trackers or telemetry bloatware.
