Key Architecture

Core Engineering Highlights

Designed to deliver reliable, unrestricted media downloading without ads, paywalls, or privacy tracking.

1,000+ Platforms Supported

Powered by the industry-standard yt-dlp extraction engine. Download video and audio from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, SoundCloud, Reddit, Vimeo, and more.

Aria2c Turbo Acceleration

Built-in multi-connection download pipeline with aria2c. Splits files into simultaneous parallel chunks for maximum network throughput and automatic connection recovery.

Resolutions Up to 8K

Pick the exact video quality you need: 360p, 480p, 720p HD, 1080p Full HD, or run an on-demand deep scan for 2K, 4K, and 8K ultra-high-definition streams.

Studio-Grade Audio & ID3 Tags

Convert videos into crystal-clear 320 kbps MP3 or lossless original AAC/M4A tracks. Automatically extracts artist info, titles, and embeds high-resolution cover art.

Android Keystore Encryption

Social session cookies for private accounts are encrypted locally using the hardware-backed Android Keystore. Sensitive tokens are never stored in plain text and expire safely after 30 days.

Built-In Floating Player

Enjoy downloads inside the app with picture-in-picture (PiP), variable speed playback (0.5x to 2x), and one-tap handover to external media players (VLC, MX Player).
