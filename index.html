Who Am I

I am a dedicated 4th-year Mechatronics Technology student at TUP-Taguig, driven by a passion for integrating advanced hardware with modern software systems. My technical expertise spans across multiple disciplines, including Full-Stack Web & Mobile Development and Systems Programming. In the physical domain, I specialize in IoT & Embedded Systems alongside Industrial Automation & Design. By uniting these fields, I design and build comprehensive, end-to-end solutions that seamlessly bridge the gap between mechanical systems and digital interfaces.

Full-Stack Web & Mobile Development

I build responsive, high-performance applications from the ground up. My expertise in React, Node.js, and Supabase allows me to architect scalable web dashboards for data monitoring, while frameworks like Flutter and React Native empower me to bring those experiences natively to mobile devices. I focus on creating seamless user experiences that communicate flawlessly with backend services.

Systems Programming

Bridging software and hardware requires robust execution and logic. I leverage C++ for high-performance algorithmic control and embedded processing, and VB.NET to develop dedicated Windows desktop interfaces for industrial monitoring. Furthermore, I utilize Python to build automation scripts, handle complex data pipelines, and train lightweight Machine Learning models to introduce predictive intelligence into mechatronic systems.

IoT & Embedded Systems

The Internet of Things is where hardware comes alive. I design and program microcontroller-based systems using the Arduino ecosystem, ESP32, and STM32. From sensor data acquisition to deploying wireless communication protocols (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, MQTT) and visual workflow programming via Node-RED, I build smart, connected edge devices that feed real-time telemetry back into cloud dashboards.

Industrial Automation & Design

My foundation lies in heavy-duty mechanical integration. I program robust Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) utilizing the Omron CX Suite to automate complex industrial machinery. Coupled with extensive CAD experience in AutoCAD and Fusion360, and rapid prototyping via 3D Printing and Bambu Studio, I am capable of designing, simulating, and fabricating complete mechatronic systems.
